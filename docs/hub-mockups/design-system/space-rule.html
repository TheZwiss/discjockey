Today, for reference: the panel swallows all the space

A

A · The page stays its default size and sits centred

List and panel keep exactly their default widths. The window's extra space becomes calm margin on both sides, like a document. Simple and predictable, but a big screen shows no more than a small one, apart from extra rows from the height.

B

B · Panel docked at the edge, list grows to a limit (my recommendation)

Like Apple Music or Finder: the panel keeps a fixed width at the right edge, and the list gets the extra width up to a comfortable maximum (about 900 px), then centres. It doesn't stretch empty rows. The extra width is used for information that's squeezed or hidden at the default size, here the playlist and why a track is in the run. On To-do it would be source, bitrate and kHz as their own columns.
