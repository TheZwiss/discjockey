Playlists

Window List State
Before: what's wrong

Every playlist is its own bordered box stacked in a column that scrolls the header and the paste field away (13 playlists already push the list off screen; the anchored-list rule forbids it). Each row carries six controls (⋮⋮, ✎, a switch, ✕, and a full-width quality bar under the name that reads as a progress bar), so the eye has no column to follow and the counts don't line up with anything. "↻ read Spotify" hides inside rows and in a loose "Saved" card, though Read Spotify lives in the page header. The prose under the list sits on the bare background. The panel shouts a 30 px sentence ("Files stay, no more updates") over a hairline checklist, and Save is an orange outline next to an equal-weight Undo: nothing says Save is the one thing to do.

A: the paste field above one anchored list (recommended)

Main: the page header with the Spotify slot (the only Read Spotify), the paste field and Add on one line, then one Surface list that fills the rest and scrolls inside, like To-do. A row is ⋮⋮ · priority · cover · folder name over "Spotify name · owner" · quality · tracks · switch · ⋯, on one grid so numbers line up down the list. The quality split becomes a short bar with its lossless count in its own column, shown only when the list is at least 680 px wide (at 1100 it gives way). Rename and Remove move into the row's ⋯ menu (and right-click); renaming edits the name in place as today. A pasted link shows the playlist it found as a card between the field and the list, with its folder name and "Add to the list". The priority rule ("a track in several playlists lands in the highest one") goes behind the ⓘ beside the list's name, and the drag hint into the list's head.

B: Add a playlist in the list's head; pasting happens in the panel

The list starts right under the header (one row more on screen); "+ Add a playlist" sits in the list's head and the panel turns into the add step (field, the found playlist, folder name, Add to the list) until you add or cancel. Cleaner main column, but adding hides what the panel was saying, and the paste field is no longer where you'd look first on an empty page.

Panel when you move playlists (pick "Big move", and try it at 1100 × 700)

The move answers two questions in order. Which folders change, and by how much? One row per changed folder, in the new list's order: the folder, a bar that grows right in orange for a folder that gets tracks and left in grey for one that gives them away (all bars on one scale, so 27 vs 3 is obvious at a glance), then its file count after the change and the difference. Which tracks go where? One list of the moving tracks, grouped by route, "Hard_Bounce → Set_04 · 12", each group's head sticking to the top while its tracks pass; every track shows its quality badge, so you see what you're moving. The same change shows in the main list too: under each affected playlist, "gets 27 more" in orange or "gives 20 away" in grey. What stays safe shrinks to one line above Save. The small one-track move ("Moved up") uses the same layout.

Panel (both): what your change does · Save as the one filled button

Nothing changed: a StatCard "tracks in several playlists" and below it the list of those tracks, grouped by the playlist that loses them ("Powertrance · 1 lands in Hard_Bounce"), so the overlap is something you see, not read. Changed: numbers first, as StatCards: new downloads and how long they take at your limit; tracks that stop updating; for a move, see Big move below. The tracks that move are the panel's list; what stays safe ("Nothing is deleted", "cue points are kept") is a quiet checklist card under them. A problem is a red NoteCard and Save stays off. Foot: Undo (secondary) and Save (filled), off until something changed. After saving, the header's Spotify slot says the new playlist isn't read yet; no extra card.
