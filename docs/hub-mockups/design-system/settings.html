Before: what's wrong

Settings is three loose cards: "Run setup again" walks the whole wizard to change one login, nothing shows what's set up, and its title sits at x 188 while every other page's sits at 754. Setup builds its own step list and titles, and "More sources" asks the DJ to pick a browser blind and press "Check and use", with the risk text as a grey box under everything. Nothing says what a login gives: a DJ with only Qobuz never learns that songs Qobuz doesn't have come in at about 130 kbps. Qobuz with a Google account needs the browser's JavaScript console, and that way no longer works at all.

After: one home for every login, and what each one gives

Settings (list + panel, like every page): the source ladder on top shows where music comes from, best first. A rung lights up in its quality colour when it works; a rung that doesn't is greyed, and the line past it is dashed: the download skips it. The one line under it says what the setup means. Below, every login and option is a row with its state and a status mark; the panel shows the picked row. An account panel says what it gives (a number with a bar: 256 against 130 kbps, and how many of your tracks came at 130), its state, then the sign-in: Discjockey looks through your browsers for a login (no macOS prompts) → Use this login; none → Sign in opens the sign-in page in your default browser and waits until you're in, then checks one song. Another browser or profile is a menu. The risk text moves behind the ⓘ. Qobuz: email and password; a Google, Apple or Facebook account sets a Qobuz password once (three steps, the first opens Qobuz's page). Setup uses the same ladder and the same panels (compact cards), with the ladder beside them so it lights up as you sign in; one filled button at a time.
