Window State
The page answers three questions, in this order

1. Are my accounts okay? The selector cards (your pick from B) carry each service's state, not just its name: today's usage as a bar and "37 of 80", and a status in colour plus shape: ✓ All good, ! 2 new warnings, ■ Paused today. You see all three at once and click the one you want.
2. How far am I? One "Today" card: what's left today (with the day bar: used · left, and the carry-over in words), and beside it the backlog with its answer: "120 tracks · about 3 days at 60 a day" (or "✓ done today"). Under it, the last 14 days, each bar coloured by how fast that day was (green normal, yellow faster, red risky), with your limit as a dashed line.
3. Is something wrong, and what should I do? The Warning signs list. New ones are marked NEW, say when they happened, and when they clear ("clears in 11 days"). Got it marks them as seen: the card and the sidebar dot go quiet until a new one arrives; seen ones stay, dimmed, until they clear. When the signs point at going too fast (rejected downloads, failed searches, preview-only files, bot checks), a line above them suggests a slower limit with a button that sets it in the panel (you still Save).

The panel is the decision: change the limit, see what it does, Save

Cause and effect sit together now. The daily limit as a big number with its field, then a risk scale in five equal steps (careful · normal · faster · fast · risky, green → yellow → red) with the handle on it and the saved value as a tick. Equal steps, because "faster: 61–79" would be a 20 px sliver on a 1–150 track. Under it, the verdict in the step's colour, then what it does: days until the backlog is done and how long one run takes, each with "instead of …" in green when it gets better. Extra just for today below, then Back · Save (the one filled button) in the foot. The honest note sits behind the ⓘ by the verdict.

Design-rule change this needs

Today the rules say green, yellow and red mean quality only. This page adds a second, separate job: risk (green safe, yellow noticeable, red risky), used only on the risk scale, the verdict, the 14-day bars and a service's state. It goes into the decision log with the reason: grey can't show "how safe is this", and a traffic light is what everyone reads.

Try it (this preview is live)

Drag the risk scale in the panel: the handle, the number, the colour, the verdict and the orange line on the 14-day chart follow your pointer 1:1; "Backlog done in" and "One run takes" are the engine's answer, so they follow every 150 ms and dim while they catch up. In New warnings, press Got it: the warnings dim, NEW goes, the card turns quiet and the sidebar dot fades, in one moment. Try 40 a day moves the limit for you; Save stays yours. All good is now one quiet line instead of six green ticks (the checks are behind its ⓘ). The verdicts are in plainer words.
