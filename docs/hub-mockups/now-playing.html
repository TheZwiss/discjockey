Now playing: global controls for track previews (issue 21)

Decided with you: play/pause, previous, next and shuffle within the list the preview started from (a snapshot: Library = the folder with its filter, search and sort; To-do = its rows that have a file). When a track ends the next one plays (shuffle order when on); it stops after the last. Wrong-song clips and the upgrade compare are checks inside one track: they pause the player, they never show in it.

Before: the sound follows you, nothing else does
After · the window gets a foot

The sidebar and a 44 px bar along the bottom become one L-shaped frame, one material with no seam where they meet; the pages sit inside it. The line between page and bar is the position: the played part runs in orange along the page's lower edge, and you scrub right on it. Left: the track and the list it plays from (a click goes back to that list, track selected), starting where the sidebar's items start. Middle: ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ between the time played and the length, centred on the window. Right: shuffle and stop. It is there only while something plays, and costs exactly the 44 px today's sync bar costs.

Dashboard at 1100 × 700 while a sync runs: one bar, not two. The sync's status moves into the bar's right end (a click opens Sync, as today). Today a sync and a player would stack 88 px.
Dashboard at 2560 × 1440: the same 44 px; the track stays at the left edge under the sidebar, the controls in the middle, shuffle and stop at the right.
Hide → show again

Hide player (⌄, next to Stop) folds the bar into the frame's corner: it slides left into the sidebar and becomes a Now playing row at the sidebar's foot, above Settings, and the page gets its 44 px back. The row keeps ⏯ and the title, with the position as a thin line under it. Show player (⌃, or a click on the title) unfolds the bar out of the corner again, the same path back. Hidden stays hidden until you show it; a new preview just changes the row.

Hidden, at 1100 × 700: no bar, the row in the sidebar.
Hidden while a sync runs: the bar shows only the sync, as today.
Every state of the bar

The layout never changes between states: a control that can't act turns grey, it isn't removed, so nothing jumps.
